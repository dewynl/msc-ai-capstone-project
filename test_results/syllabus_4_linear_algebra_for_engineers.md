# Linear Algebra for Engineers
**[Semester]**

## Course Description

Mathematical foundations of linear algebra with engineering applications

## Additional Course Information

Students will translate real-world scenarios into mathematical systems through structured problem analysis and variable identification exercises - Students will solve systems of linear equations in two and three variables by applying graphing, substitution, and elimination methods with 90% accuracy on computational problems - Teachers in Mathematics with relevant prerequisites


## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

• Students will solve systems of linear equations in two and three variables by applying graphical, substitution, and elimination methods with 90% accuracy on practice problems
• Students will translate real-world scenarios into mathematical systems through structured problem analysis and variable identification exercises
• Students will solve systems of two linear equations in two variables by applying graphing, substitution, and elimination methods with 85% accuracy on computational problems
• Students will analyze real-world scenarios and construct appropriate systems of linear equations through guided practice with mixture, motion, and business optimization problems

## Target Audience

Undergraduate students in Mathematics with appropriate prerequisites and foundational knowledge.

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

• Students will conduct comprehensive exploratory data analysis by implementing systematic data profiling techniques to identify missing values, outliers, and data quality issues
• Students will create effective data visualizations through hands-on practice with matplotlib, seaborn, and plotly to reveal patterns, correlations, and distributions in real datasets
• Students will conduct comprehensive univariate analysis by creating appropriate statistical summaries and visualizations for different data types
• Students will identify multivariate relationships and patterns through correlation analysis, scatter plots, and advanced visualization techniques

## Target Audience

Graduate students in Data Science with appropriate prerequisites and foundational knowledge.

# Digital Marketing Strategy
**[Semester]**

## Course Description

Modern digital marketing approaches including social media, SEO, and analytics

## Additional Course Information

Students will create comprehensive project scope statements and WBS structures by applying PMBOK Guide processes 5.1-5.6 to case study projects - Students will evaluate and control scope changes through role-playing exercises involving scope change requests and stakeholder negotiations - Student will create comprehensive risk registers by applying structured risk identification techniques including brainstorming, expert interviews, and checklist analysis Target Audience: Professional students in Business with relevant prerequisites


## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

• Students will create comprehensive project scope statements and WBS structures by applying PMBOK Guide processes 5.1-5.6 to case study projects
• Students will evaluate and control scope changes through role-playing exercises involving scope change requests and stakeholder negotiations
• Students will create comprehensive risk registers by applying structured risk identification techniques including brainstorming, expert interviews, and checklist analysis
• Students will evaluate and prioritize project risks through qualitative risk analysis using probability and impact matrices with numerical scoring

## Target Audience

Professional students in Business with appropriate prerequisites and foundational knowledge.

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

• Students will diagnose follower development levels using the Situational Leadership competence and commitment matrix by analyzing real workplace scenarios and team member behaviors
• Students will demonstrate appropriate leadership style selection through structured role-playing exercises that simulate directing, coaching, supporting, and delegating situations
• Students will diagnose follower readiness levels by assessing competence and commitment using the Situational Leadership diagnostic framework
• Students will select appropriate leadership styles (directing, coaching, supporting, delegating) through analysis of specific workplace scenarios and team member needs
